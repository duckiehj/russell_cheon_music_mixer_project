functionality - music mixer

Basic Functionality:

drag an animal icon from a map to the band zones

once dragged, the icon becomes or is replaced by a band member

audio plays once dragged


Characters:
There are two types of characters.
First, users need to select an instrument for the DJ who plays the main song.
After that, they will drag in 4 other band members who will sing the accompanied noises
In total there are 5 slots (1 for main DJ, 4 for others)
